About LyricView Pro

LyricView Pro is a church presentation software family designed for displaying lyrics, Bible verses, schedules, and media during worship services.

The LyricView Pro family includes multiple generations and editions, from the original V1 and V2 releases, through V3 and the advanced LyricView Pro X, to the latest LyricView Pro X Studio.

LyricView Pro X provides expanded presentation, media, Bible, schedule, and remote control capabilities. LyricView Pro X Studio builds upon the X generation with a dedicated Studio Editor workflow for creating advanced slide compositions, multimedia presentations, and LyricView Pro project files.

LyricView Pro is designed to provide churches and worship teams with a flexible presentation workflow for preparing and operating live services.
